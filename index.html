Biography

Zyeem Bin Zia is the owner, founder and chief executive of ZyeemZia™, and the engineer behind everything it publishes. A software engineer and systems architect based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, he runs the studio as an independent operation that designs, builds and ships its own software end to end — the applications, the servers, the course material and the sites they are delivered on. There is no team behind the name; the catalogue is the record of one engineer working across the whole stack.

His training sits across two disciplines. A Master of Science in Computer Science follows a Bachelor of Science in Electrical, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and the combination shows in what he chooses to build: software that lives close to hardware and to the physics of the field — coordinate systems, signal and telemetry, sensors, radio and timing — rather than software that assumes a data centre is always one hop away. That foundation is extended by professional specialisations from Google, IBM and a number of academic institutions, across networking, cybersecurity, machine learning and project leadership.

The work divides into three strands. The first is a catalogue of offline-first Android applications, written in Flutter and Dart and published on Google Play for users worldwide: document and office tooling with on-device OCR, a format-agnostic coordinate conversion engine with Kalman-filtered route tracking, signal and Morse training suites, precision telemetry and vector analysis, and mapping utilities that keep working with the radio off. The second is ZyeemZia™ Academy, a free certification campus of twenty-four courses and more than a thousand lessons, spanning networking, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, project and service management, law and five languages — each with an interactive course, a printable book, a server-graded exam and a credential anyone can verify. The third is research: two papers now under review at IEEE Embedded Systems Letters: a reinforcement-learning deception system that protects a live post-quantum IoT gateway, built on ML-KEM-768 over an ESP32, against reconnaissance, downgrade and denial-of-service attacks; and a measurement study of post-quantum key establishment over Bluetooth Low Energy between a smartphone and an ESP32, which finds that link parameters, not the cryptography, decide how long it takes. Extended journal versions of both are in preparation.

A consistent engineering position runs through all of it. His applications do not require an account, do not carry advertising, and do not transmit the user's work anywhere: documents, scans, routes and progress stay on the device that produced them. The courses are free, in full, with no tier above them. The stated reason is practical rather than ideological — software built for people working at the edge of coverage has to assume the connection will fail, and the cheapest way to protect data is never to collect it.

Alongside the core studio he runs ZBZGames™, an interactive entertainment branch producing offline simulation and arcade titles and a free browser arcade of forty original games, ZQuiz, a live multiplayer quiz platform for classrooms and teams, ZyeemZia™ AI, a team of free AI agents that guides visitors through the whole estate, reads what they attach and lets anyone design their own AI coworker, and ZBZAI™, a free offline AI engine that installs from one file on a Windows PC and serves a whole office, school or home network: it reads office files and hands back the edited ones, adds office coworkers and a studio for video, music and pictures, and grows from the organisation’s own documents without anything leaving the building. His current research interest is the intersection he keeps returning to: AI-driven cyber defence for cyber-physical and post-quantum systems.

How the work is built

Offline first

Connectivity is treated as an optional extra, never a dependency. If it works without a network, it works everywhere.

On the device

Documents, scans, routes and progress stay where they were created. The cheapest data to protect is data never collected.

No gate

No accounts, no advertising, no paid tier above the free one. Every course and every credential is free in full.

Built in house

Application, server, course material and site are written by one engineer, which keeps the whole stack answerable to one standard.
